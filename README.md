## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **PCB Design Engineer** (B.E. ECE, 2025) who turns schematics into manufacturable boards. I care about clean schematics, constraint-driven layout and documentation that fabrication and assembly teams can trust.

- 🎯 Focused on **signal integrity, power integrity and DFM-aware layout**
- 🧰 Daily drivers: **Cadence Allegro PCB Designer** and **OrCAD Capture**
- 🔁 Also fluent in **Altium Designer** and **KiCad**
- 📐 Comfortable across the full flow: schematic, stackup, layout, fabrication outputs
- 🗣️ English & Tamil

## ⚡ What I Do

| Stage | Focus |
|---|---|
